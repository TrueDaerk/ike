# Concepts

The mental model behind the interface: how the workspace is arranged, how the
editor's modes work, and what IKE considers a project.

!!! info "This section is being written"
    Progress is tracked in

Planned pages:

- **Panes, tabs and layouts** — splitting the workspace, resizing and moving panes with the mouse, saved layouts, and how layouts persist per project.
- **The modal editor** — normal, insert and visual modes; motions, operators, text objects, registers, undo and redo.
- **Projects and sessions** — what the project root is, what gets restored when you reopen a project, and how crash recovery works.
- **Commands and the palette** — why almost everything is a command, and how the palette, the menu bar and keybindings are three views of the same registry.
